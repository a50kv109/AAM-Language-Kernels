# Japanese Language Kernel (L2_JA)

## Status
- **Kernel status**: PROPOSED / INITIAL BASELINE
- **Implementation maturity**: Limited to documentation, pattern registry, and local validation checks.
- **Not claimed**: a fully benchmarked or production-ready Japanese language engine.

## Topology
- **Grammar (L1)**: Core syntax, particles, and basic morphologies for an introductory baseline.
- **Kanji (L2)**: Multi-dimensional lexical mapping and context-dependent readings as a research layer.
- **Engineering (L3)**: SOL anchors mapping for SOURCE, CONSTRAINT, FLOW, OBSERVATION, etc.
- **Context (L4)**: Omission resolution and ENGINEERED_IGNORANCE handling as a proposed high-context model.

## Research extension layer
- **Google / Mozc-inspired research modules**: Additional candidate-generation, surrounding-text, technical-writing, and typography notes are catalogued under the existing Japanese kernel for research and architectural exploration.
- **Epistemic handling**: These modules are retained as `SOURCE_SUPPORTED`, `PROPOSED`, or `RESEARCH_ONLY` materials and are not promoted to verified runtime status without direct evidence.
