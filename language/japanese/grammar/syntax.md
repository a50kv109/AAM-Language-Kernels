# Syntax

- **Clause structure**: Japanese commonly uses a clause pattern with verb-final positioning, but a strict universal `SOV` rule is too rigid for all registers and contexts.
- **Modifiers**: Modifying phrases often appear before the noun they qualify, but surface ordering should be treated as a common pattern rather than a fixed universal law.
- **Ellipsis**: Subjects and objects are often omitted when contextually understood; `ENGINEERED_IGNORANCE` applies, and unresolved actors must be marked explicitly instead of guessed.
