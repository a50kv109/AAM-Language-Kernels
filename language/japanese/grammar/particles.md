# Particles

- **は (wa)**: Topic marker. Introduces known information.
- **が (ga)**: Subject marker. Identifies new actors.
- **を (wo)**: Direct object marker for transitive verbs.
- **に (ni)**: Target of motion, specific time, or location of existence.
- **で (de)**: Context of action, means, or instrument.
- **へ (e)**: Direction of motion.
- **も (mo)**: Inclusion (also, too).
