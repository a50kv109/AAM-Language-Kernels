## Lexical cautions
- *piano* can mean a dynamic, the instrument (*pianoforte*), or an ordinary adjective/adverb. Domain context is essential.
- *andantino* has historically variable tempo interpretation; avoid a rigid mapping.
- *ritenuto* and *ritardando* can differ in the nature of tempo change; do not collapse them into one label.
- *tenuto* can concern duration, emphasis, or both depending on notation and context.
- Italian score vocabulary includes conventionalized terms whose performance meaning is not always a literal translation.
