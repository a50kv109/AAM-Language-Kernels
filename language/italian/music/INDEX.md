# Italian Music-Language Layer

**Layer ID:** IT-MUSIC-01  
**Status:** INITIAL CORPUS  
**Parent kernel:** IT:13

## Purpose
This layer captures Italian lexical and grammatical patterns frequently found in Western classical music scores. It supports reading, classification and cautious interpretation of score directions; it does not decide artistic performance.

## Files
- [Musical grammar patterns](musical_grammar_patterns.md)
- [Core musical vocabulary](musical_vocabulary.md)

## Coverage
1. Tempo and tempo modification
2. Character and expression
3. Dynamics and dynamic transitions
4. Articulation and phrasing
5. Common adverbial and imperative constructions
6. Ellipsis and abbreviated score directions

## Interpretation rules
- Preserve the original Italian wording.
- Separate literal meaning from conventional musical function.
- Do not infer an exact metronome value from a qualitative tempo word alone.
- Treat instrument, passage, period, composer and surrounding directions as relevant context.
- Keep uncertain, damaged or abbreviated text unresolved until evidence supports a reading.
- Distinguish Italian directions from terms borrowed from French, German or other languages.

## Status discipline
This is a curated starting set, not a complete grammar or validated automatic parser. Patterns require authentic score examples and review before becoming canonical rules.
