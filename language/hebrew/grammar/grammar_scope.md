# Grammar Research Scope

**Status:** RESEARCH_ONLY / NOT_VERIFIED. The source packages contain no Hebrew grammar rules or examples.

Future source-backed coverage should distinguish:

- clause and phrase structure by historical period and register;
- particles and prepositions in context;
- verbal morphology and agreement;
- nominal agreement and construct relations;
- Modern Hebrew usage from Biblical, Mishnaic, Talmudic, and Medieval evidence;
- written, spoken, and marked registers.

This inventory makes no claim that one word order, paradigm, or historical grammar applies to all Hebrew texts. No syntax tree is generated by the current utility.
