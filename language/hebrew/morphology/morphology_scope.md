Research topics to address with cited references and reviewed examples:

- Root-and-pattern morphology, including triliteral roots where applicable.
- Binyanim and the limits of treating them as a simple voice or valency mapping.
- Inflectional forms and the distinction between derivation and inflection.
- Gender and number marking, agreement, and exceptions.
- Construct state (סמיכות) and its syntax/semantics.
- Weak, geminate, and irregular roots.
- Pointed and unpointed forms and lexical ambiguity.
