# Semantics Research Scope

**Status:** RESEARCH_ONLY / NOT_VERIFIED.

The supplied materials do not include a source-backed Hebrew semantic lexicon. Future records should keep the following distinct:

- attested lexical senses and their contexts;
- polysemy and homography;
- historical or register-specific meanings;
- cultural concepts and interpretive traditions;
- modern AAM semantic mappings.

The ZIC registry is maintained separately as an author-reported AAM extension. Its scores and labels are not objective linguistic measurements and are not used by the runtime.
