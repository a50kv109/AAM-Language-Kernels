## Research discipline

The Chinese kernel distinguishes between:

- primary-source text;
- source-text availability states;
- literal translation;
- semantic explanation;
- procedural description;
- modern formalization;
- interpretation notes;
- unresolved questions;
- provenance metadata.

This distinction is preserved in the canonical pattern registry and in the provenance notes. The `ZH_PAT_003` record remains explicitly marked as source-unavailable and awaiting verification rather than silently substituting explanatory prose for a missing quotation.
